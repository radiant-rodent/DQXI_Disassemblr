## Open problems / not verified

- `0x00800002` and `0x00800402` are not true RAM variables, but rather 32-bit CTR-SDK static buffer descriptors: `IPC_Desc_StaticBuffer(0x200, 0)` and `IPC_Desc_StaticBuffer(0x200, 1)`. Because their literal pool words happened to equal `0x00800002` and `0x00800402` (which falls numerically into the BSS virtual address space), the batch generation script included them as globals. They have been documented and typed as `u32` with `maybe_` prefix and confidence 0.40 to explain their exact IPC encoding.
- `Svc_GetTableRow` (0x001D5F74) was originally given a `Svc_` prefix due to its internal call to `__cxa_guard_acquire`, but caller analysis revealed that it is actually a model face animation frame table accessor called exclusively by `maybe_Model_UpdateFaceAnimation` (0x001D5AE0). The table at `0x007C3448` and guard at `0x007A3510` have been named accordingly.
- The concrete C++ class names for RTTI tokens `0x007C43D8` and `0x007C489C` are not directly visible in string literals; their roles are deduced from their usages in `maybe_Menu_TalkParty_IsActive` and `Actor2D_HasPatternResource`.
