## What was done

Named and documented all 150 functions in the batch. For each entry the readable C (or
`decomp/readable/recovered/` for the two merged entry points) was read together with:

- callers/callees from the Ghidra index and the readable C,
- literal strings resolved from `original/exefs/code.decompressed.bin`
  (`file offset = address - 0x00100000`),
- globals and vtables (vtable slots were resolved back to their containing functions to tie
  the small static constructors to the classes built nearby),
- `symbols.json`, `decomp/native_names.json` and the Squirrel-facing names for cross-checks.

## Method notes / decisions

- `std_strncat` (0x00100990) is confirmed by exact strncat semantics (scan dest to NUL, copy at
  most `count` bytes, always terminate) and 12 callers, not by a symbol.
- The eight small `0x0016xxxx` constructors are static-init style factories. Their vtables were
  resolved (e.g. `g_0016098c` → 0x758a60 whose slots 0x0018FC30/0x0018FC34/0x0018FC84 belong to
  `0x0018F974`), which is why they are tied to a class rather than left as `maybe_InitObjectNNN`.
  The class names themselves remain guesses.
- The `win_*` constructors from 0x0027C56C to 0x0027DF28 are named from their own literal layer
  names (`win_dougu`, `win_enemy`, `win_equip1`, `win_party`, `win_skill`, `win_tokugi`,
  `win_command`, `win_message`, `win_training`, `win_sakusen1..3`, `win_renkei1..3`,
  `win_irekae1..4`), so those names are the most reliable in the batch.
- `0x0024107C` was re-read from its strings and renamed `Ui_SetupAdventureLogWindow` (play time,
  wins, monsters, medals, smithing, items, recipes, title), replacing the earlier
  "monster album" guess.
- For the two merged entries: the Ghidra/recovered body at 0x00161974 actually references the
  literal pool at 0x00181D54, i.e. the recovered listing mixes both entry points. It is used as
  the colour/effect updater body for both 0x00161974 and 0x00181C94; 0x00181C94 keeps the
  `Field_UpdateEffectParams` name and 0x00161974 is the twin `maybe_Field_UpdateEffectColor`.
- `0x00148E28` was renamed `maybe_Map_CheckCellBlocked`: its body walks 6-byte collision entries
  with an id and flag bytes, consults a per-id blocking table and builds a 24-byte cell node with
  coordinates shifted left by 3. The earlier encounter-monster interpretation is not supported.

## Open problems

1. **84 of 150 entries are below 0.5 confidence** (average 0.49). Most are the 0x0016xxxx
   static constructors, the 0x001C–0x0020 model/effect helpers and the 0x0028xxxx UI region,
   where the decompiled bodies are small wrappers or heavily merged and no literal strings exist.
2. **No runtime confirmation.** Names were not checked against the Squirrel bindings for these
   exact addresses; `native_names.json` did not contain them, so most evidence is structural
   (callers, vtables, literals) rather than behavioural.
3. The eight vtable-derived class names (0x0016096C, 0x00161160, 0x00161DC4, 0x00162084,
   0x00162278, 0x00162758, 0x00162DA8, 0x00162DE8) identify the right *class link* but the
   subsystem word in the name is still a guess.
4. `0x002826B0` (`maybe_Ui_UpdateScrollOffset`) and `0x00257704`
   (`maybe_Ui_UpdateTransition`) are the weakest functional readings; both act on UI-like
   objects but the decompiled float/timer logic could also be animation or camera state.
