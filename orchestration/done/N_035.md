## 2. Method

1. **Merged name map.** `symbols.json`, `symbols_recovered.json`, `decomp/native_names.json` and every
   other `orchestration/naming/out/N_*.json` into one address→name table. This matters a lot here:
   N_007 (`Sound_Decoder_Init`, `Sound_VoicePool_AllocateBuffers`, `Sound_SetOutputMode`,
   `Sound_AdpcmChannel_InitTable`), N_008 (`Sound_StreamPlayer_Setup/Release`) and the immediately
   adjacent N_034 (`0x00511774-0x00548150`: `Sound_System_Shutdown`, `Sound_StreamThread_Main`,
   `Sound_Archive_OpenEntryStream`, `maybe_Sound_Stream_*`) supply the anchors that make the rest
   readable.
2. **Caller index** over all 18,159 `decomp/readable/**/*.c` bodies (call-token scan), with the
   readable-C header stripped first. That strip matters: the header line `Symbol: func_XXXXXXXX`
   counts as a self-call under a naive scan and made ~30 functions look "called by themselves", which
   is exactly the kind of noise that hides the 60 caller-less functions in this batch.
3. **Literal resolution**: every `g_`/`s_` token was dereferenced against
   `original/exefs/code.decompressed.bin` (image base 0x00100000) and classified as ASCII string,
   UTF-16 string, float or word. This is where the format magics came from.
4. **Assembly** (`asm/text/text_00540048.s`) for the functions Ghidra merged into their neighbours.
   `decomp/ghidra/functions_index.json` was used to find *which* function swallowed an entry point
   (e.g. `0x0054E120` → inside `FUN_0054E084`, `0x0054E304` → inside `FUN_0054E2B0`); the bodies were
   then read from the disassembly, which is 2 instructions each.
5. **Bottom-up**: leaf setters/getters and the field readers first, then the objects that own them,
   then the driver tick and the command executor.

## 3. Cross-cutting discoveries (reusable — the important part)

### (a) The 3DS CWAV wave format anchors the whole first half

`0x0054944C` compares `0x56415743` = **"CWAV"**, then resolves **"INFO"** (`0x4F464E49`) and
**"DATA"** (`0x41544144`) through `0x00663308`/`0x006632A0`. Everything downstream follows:
`0x00549FFC`–`0x0054B12C` is the wave cache/decoder, `0x0065EF6C`/`0x006605B4` plus the seven
tag readers `0x0065EA1C`–`0x0065EBDC` are the INFO-block field readers, and the decoded waves are
prefixed with a 0x20-byte in-memory header tagged **"CIWV"** (`0x0054A16C`). This also independently
confirms N_007's reading of `0x0054B608` as the DSP channel setup (32/33-entry channel arrays).

### (b) The sound object model (top-down)

```
SoundDriver        0x0054D8C0 tick / 0x0054D93C tick-without-profiling
                   monitor +0x1C4, deferred-release lists +0x1D4/+0x1E0, flag +0x1F6
SoundMixer         0x00550C3C walks the voice list in 3 passes; voice pool alloc 0x00550A0C
SoundVoice         0x0054C664..0x0054D834  1-2 DSP streams, 3x4 float pan matrix, priority
SoundSeqPlayer     0x00552914..0x00555B60  16 channel objects at +0x90, 2 slots at +0x0..0x10,
                   note handles at +0x290, BPM->samples at 0x005549EC (1/60000)
SoundSeqChannel    0x0054E130..0x0054EE94  note list +0x130, five envelope pairs +0x8C..+0xA8
SoundSeqNote       0x0055614C..0x0055668C  voice +0x1A8, state +0x98 (4 = finished)
SoundPlayer        0x0054F278..0x00550744  the streaming path: open -> wait -> decode -> play
```

The hierarchy is *proven*, not guessed, by the **channel vtable at `0x0073D2E8`**: sixteen
`{code pointer, 0}` pairs. Slot 0 = `0x0054EBA4`; slot 1 = `0x0054E084` (sets the per-note fade
target to 0x00/0xFF over N frames); slot 7 = `0x0054E304` (`vstr s0,[r0,#0x108]; strb r1,[r0,#0xFE]`);
slot 9 = `0x0054E120` (`strb r1,[r0,#0x83]`); slot 10 = `0x0054E128` (`strb r1,[r0,#0xF4]`);
slot 11 = `0x0054E320` (`strb r1,[r0,#0xF0]`). The six mask-broadcast helpers
(`0x00554CB0`, `0x00554DC0`, `0x00554FB8`, `0x00554E80`, `0x00554F1C`, `0x00555048`) were named from
those slots, and the byte/float setters were read straight out of the assembly.

### (c) `0x00548808` is the consumer of the sound command queue

It walks a singly linked list (`next` at +0, **opcode byte at +4**, target at +0x10) and dispatches
opcodes `2..0x3A`. All 48 callees sit in 0x0054Exxx–0x00556xxx; opcode 0x0E receives exactly the
3+13-word payload that `0x00548F90` enqueues, opcodes 0x0D/0x0F match `0x005490DC`/`0x00549154`, and
opcode 0x3A uses the same queue helpers that N_007's `Sound_SetOutputMode` (`0x0054F604`) uses when it
enqueues a 0x3A node. The producers allocate nodes with `0x0011B57C` and submit with `0x0011B89C`.

### (d) The sequence interpreter is at 0x661640–0x662C90

`0x00662C90` is a textbook MIDI variable-length reader (`result = (b & 0x7F) | result << 7` while the
0x80 bit is set; case 2 = big-endian u16). `0x00662714` reads 0xA2/0xA3 prefix opcodes from a byte
cursor, and `0x006626D8` is the six-argument thunk into `Sound_SeqChannel_StartNote` — i.e. the
note-on callback.

### (e) Duplicates worth knowing about

* `0x00549288` == `0x00556E68` (byte-identical request canceller).
* `0x0054D8C0` == `0x0054D93C` minus the frame-profiling block (dev/release twins).
* The "cancel work by owner" helper exists **three times**: `0x005480C4` (N_034's
  `maybe_Sound_Stream_CancelWorkByOwner`), `0x005514B4` and `0x00551B58` (mine, `...A`/`...B`).
* `0x00555048`/`0x00554F1C`/`0x00554E80` call entry points *inside* `FUN_0054E084`/`FUN_0054E2B0`;
  Ghidra's function sizes there are wrong (it swallowed 4 small vtable methods).

## 5. Confidence and honesty

* Mean confidence **0.445**; 24 entries are not `maybe_` (1 ≥ 0.60, 23 at exactly 0.50–0.55), 126 are
  `maybe_`.
* What is *proven* and can be cited by others:
  `Sound_Cwav_Open` (0.70 — three magics read out of the literal pool), `std_Int64MulDiv` (0.55),
  `Sound_Seq_ReadVarLenArg` (0.55 — explicit 7-bit varint loop),
  `Sound_SeqChannel_StartNote`/`Stop`/`KillNotes`/`Dtor`, `Sound_Voice_StopStreams`/`UpdateVolumes`,
  `Sound_Mixer_BuildVolumeMatrix` (0.50 — 12-float output, 0..1 clamps, 8-way speaker mode switch),
  the four `Sound_SeqChannel_*` note/fade paths, and the movie frame ring trio (a plain head/tail
  ring with the wrap arithmetic visible).
* What is **not** verified:
  * The six channel-broadcast helpers are certain about *which field they write*
    (`+0x83`, `+0xF0`, `+0xF4`, `+0x108`, `+0xFE`, `+0x7B`) and not about *what the game calls that
    field*, so they are named `SetChannelField83/FieldF0/FieldF4/Float108` rather than guessed.
  * `0x005543B0`, `0x005549EC`, `0x00554BC4` and `0x00552E3C` operate on a 0x2200-byte sequencer
    struct whose field meanings are inferred from the values that flow through them (the 1/60000 BPM
    pair, the 3+13-word parameter block); 0.42–0.50.
  * `0x0054D8C0`/`0x0054D93C` cannot be *proven* to be the per-frame tick because neither has a caller
    in the image; they are reached by pointer, most likely from the sound thread entry that N_034
    named `Sound_StreamThread_Main` (`0x005476B0`).
  * The two `Sound_Profile_*` buffer setters are 0.49: the field triple `+0x1B8/+0x1BC/+0x1C0` is
    shared with the tick code, but calling it "profiling" is an interpretation of a 101-bucket
    histogram plus an 8-byte record ring.
  * `0x0055668C` is still a 0.35 structural guess (a half-open interval overlap over a note's
    sub-notes) and has no caller.
  * The CECD/StreetPass identification of `0x00658618`–`0x00658B28` is **inherited** from the callee
    names that earlier batches gave (`maybe_Svc_Cecd_WriteRawFile`, `Svc_Cecd_DeleteDispatch`,
    `maybe_Ui_IconCache_EnsureService`); I only established the retry/local-`ERROR   `-record
    mechanics, not the service identity.
  * `0x0054CE30`, `0x00554E80`, `0x00554DC0` were 0.30–0.35 before the vtable/asm evidence arrived
    and were raised to 0.45 afterwards; they would be the first entries to re-check if a later lane
    finds the channel class definition.

   this is a 30-minute job that would give semantic names to the six "broadcast" helpers in this
   batch for free.
4. **The same treatment for the 0x0054F278-0x00550744 `SoundPlayer`**: 15 functions are named by their
   observable effect; a pass over the four `0x005[12]xxx` track helpers (`0x5164FC`, `0x5166E4`,
   `0x51658C`, `0x519490`, `0x519674`, `0x51871C`, `0x518750`) would pin their real meaning.
5. **Record the queue opcode table.** `0x00548808`'s opcodes 2..0x3A are now each mapped to a handler
   address in this report and in the JSON; writing that table into `docs/` would let script modders
   reason about what the game sends to the sound thread.
6. The `0x0011B7E8`/`0x0011B57C`/`0x0011B89C` command-buffer triple (allocate node → fill → commit,
   with a 1 s blocking wait at `0x0011CC94`/`0x000F4240`) is used by the sound pool, the archive I/O
   and the stream work queue alike. It deserves one documented name in the merge
   (`std_CommandBuffer_*`) instead of being treated as three sound functions.
