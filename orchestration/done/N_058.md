## Method (reproducible)

Scratch files: `%TEMP%/opencode/n058_batch1..5.py`, `n058_names.json`,
`n058_callers.json`, `n058_overview.txt`, `n058_xrefs.json`. Nothing was added to the repo.

1. **Name map** — merged `symbols.json`, `symbols_recovered.json`,
   `decomp/native_names.json` and all 53 sibling `out/N_*.json`.
2. **Callers/callees** — built an inverted call index over all 18,159 files in
   `decomp/ghidra/functions`, plus a raw-address xref scan (data references included).
   82 of 150 are not referenced textually: they are virtual methods whose vtable lives in
   data, which is itself evidence for the class families.
3. **Strings/constants** — every `s_*`/`PTR_*`/`DAT_*` literal-pool word was resolved
   against `original/exefs/code.decompressed.bin` (base 0x00100000), so the strings quoted
   in the evidence are read from the retail binary, not from Ghidra's truncated symbol names.
4. **Truncated bodies** — seven functions have `WARNING: Subroutine does not return` because
   Ghidra mis-modelled 0x0029126C; their legacy `decomp/functions/func_*.c` text was read to
   recover the tail (this matters for 0x3DE464, 0x3E0F70, 0x3E6B40, 0x3E7DD0, 0x3EC23C and
   the 0x3ECA7C/0x3ECAC0 pair).
5. **Scripts** — `game_assets/scripts/` has no binding for any of these addresses; all 150
   are internal native/vtable entries, so the scripts gave no confirmation.

What is **proven** (and why):

- The Effect2D manager (0x3DB7C4-0x3DC7C8) is proven by five distinct file-format strings
  (`effect2d/%s.pack`, `common_effect.pack`, `effect2d_packinfo.epi`, `%s.bspa`, `Start`)
  plus the 0x21-slot / 0x10-byte slot layout shared by six of the functions.
- The animation-binding helpers (0x3DFF34, 0x3E002C, 0x3E0088, 0x3E00CC, 0x3E032C,
  0x3E0454) are proven by their call graph (110 / 131 / 73 UI-window callers) and by the
   ui2d-style bind path 0x53D528 -> 0x535D3C -> 0x53681C -> 0x53649C -> 0x5363E8.
- The field actions are proven by their animation literals: `Ascent` (0x3E4614),
  `BreakObject` (0x3E47BC), `Decent` (0x3E4888), `Dragon_Jump` (0x3E5058),
  `Rope_Up/Down`, `Ladder_Up/Down`, `OpenItemBox`, `Hell_TackleLoop/End`, `Narrow_Nut`.
- The smith detail functions are proven by `power_num0` and the four `menu_kaji*` strings.
- `Field_NpcEcstacyAction_Ctor` is proven by its caller: `Field_NpcPlayGesture` passes the
  literal `"ecstsy"` for gesture id 2.

What is **not** verified:

- The owner of the 0x3D9E1E slot table. It is a 10-slot (id, colour) table with 0xFF
  sentinels, a palette lookup through 0x3DA43C/0x63F3F0/0x5F9650 and 2D/3D position picks.
  It could be map name tags, the Dive questionnaire or the message speaker table; the names
  deliberately say only "slot table" and the confidence is 0.25-0.40.
- The 0x3D8D74/0x3D91F0 family is called a "talk balloon" because the only caller is
  `Field_SetupTalkParams` and the bodies build a 2D object plus open a message
  (0x255268/0x253290). The engine's own term is probably *fukidashi* (see the existing
  `FukidashiMessage` symbol at 0x150FD0).
- Field-action class names are inferred from their literal pools, not from RTTI. The
  vtables are known (e.g. 0x3E45A4, 0x3E481C, 0x3E5724) but not their class names.
- Seven bodies are decompiler-damaged or argument-register-lossy (0x3DCC3C, 0x3DE464,
  0x3E0F70, 0x3E6B40, 0x3E7DD0, 0x3EC23C, 0x3ECA7C/0x3ECAC0); their names rely on the
  legacy decompilation tails and the surrounding call graph.
- Nothing was executed, rebuilt or run on hardware/emulator. Per the task, the naming lane
  is exempt from `tests/run_all.py` and `tools/verify_project.py`.
