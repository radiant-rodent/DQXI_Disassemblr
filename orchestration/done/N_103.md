## 3. Load-bearing findings (worth reusing in the merge)

1. **`0x0010F6CC` is `operator delete`** and **`0x00193E44`/`0x00193E40` install the shared base
   vtable `0x758AE0`**. That pair identifies eight otherwise opaque 8–16 byte bodies in this batch
   as *scalar deleting destructors* (`0x1D0B70`, `0x1D10B0`, `0x1DC234`, `0x1DC418`, `0x1E0D2C`,
   `0x1E1238`, `0x1E1B70`, `0x213128`, `0x215B10`, `0x22741C`, `0x227CC8`).
2. **`0x1CA4D8` writes the actor model-part code table** at `+0x744..+0x74A` (record types
   2/4/5/6/8), and `0x1CA7E4` / `0x1CB744` are the two predicates that read it. Part `+0x74A ==
   0x54` (the `'T'` code) and `+0x745 ∈ {'A','B','C'}` are the visible tail/limb markers.
3. **`0x1CA880` + `0x1CC96C` + `0x1CD328` are one family**: three 0x20-byte model-variant blocks at
   `+0xB108/+0xB128/+0xB148`, selected by the flags at `+0xB0F7/+0xB0F9`, applied through vtable
   `+0x178` / `+0x18C` and reset by `0x1CD328`.
4. **`0x1EDBBC` is `Sound_ReleaseVoicesByOwner`**: four groups × 0x280-byte rows × 0x14-byte voice
   entries, with an occupancy bitmap at `+0x80` (bit per slot) and three live counters. This is the
   clearest name in the batch (confidence 0.52) and gives the sound lane the voice-slot layout.
5. **`0x1E8730` is `Menu_Senreki_BuildListEntry`** — Ghidra itself emitted the record symbol
   `s_menu_senreki_record_001e8770` (senreki = the battle-history menu); its counterpart
   `0x1E888C` re-initialises exactly the three list heads that builder fills.
6. **`0x1DAB50` is the `__pure_virtual` stub** (`0x29126C` + status 0, Ghidra "subroutine does not
   return"), the same trap `Ui_MessageWindow_CreateTextSlow` (0x1C4980) uses.
7. **`0x210A38` is not code**: the preceding function `0x2109FC` ends exactly there, and the 8 bytes
   are its literal pool (`0x0075B584`, `0x0000B18F`). Documented as data rather than a function.
8. **`0x1F16A4` / `0x1F19EC` / `0x1F1AD8` are the title-screen demo battle** that N_011 had already
   half-named (`Ui_TitleDemo_Init` 0x1F1628 / `Ui_TitleDemo_Update` 0x1F1B04). Their callees
   (`Battle_SetupUnitFromMonsterData`, `Party_Status_SetMemberValue`, `Battle_SetUnitModelVariant`,
   `Snd_CommitPendingBgmId`) were recovered by scanning BL words, because Ghidra's C for these three
   is `halt_baddata` or starts mid-function with an epilogue.
9. **`0x2213F0` (0x5D8 bytes, Thumb-2) is a model/outfit applicator**: scanning its BL words yields
   `std_memcpy`, `Model_LookupIdByKey`, `Actor_ApplyModelSetByName`, `Actor_ApplyByNameWithParts`,
   `Model_ApplyFaceTexture`, `Model_AttachSlot_Show`, `Anim_ActionSeq_Reset` and
   `Mem_AllocAligned`, plus a copy of 0x12 bytes from `record+0x120`.
10. **Camp-menu 3-slot idiom**: `0x215B10`–`0x21680A` all loop over three 0xC-byte sub-objects at
    `self+4`, calling vtable `+0x1C/+0x24/+0x28/+0x30/+0x34` (readiness) or `0x17BB7C/0x17BBFC/
    0x17BC6C/0x17BD1C/0x17BDC8` (handle/param get-set). Four of them are literal duplicates with
    different vtable slots, hence the `B`/`C`/`D`/`E` suffixes.
