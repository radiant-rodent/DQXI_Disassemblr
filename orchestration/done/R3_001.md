- **8 confirmed** (0.50–0.55): `Camera_SetFocusOffsetAndBlend`,

  `Battle_Ui_FinishActorMessage`, `Gfx_WaitForIpcIdle`, `Gfx_EntryPool_Clear`,

- **20 corrected**. Most important:
  - `maybe_Field_InitObjectTable` → **`Battle_InitWork`** (0.55). The tail initialises the exact
    battle-work fields FACTS lists (+0x4254 shared unit list, +0x428C stride-0x1C unit lists,
    +0x4330 3D / +0x47A0 2D model tables, five 0x40-byte party slots); its only caller passes
    work+0x5C, which is the battle work.
  - `maybe_Ui_RecipeBook_ChooseRecipeLine` → **`BattleAi_ChooseActionSlot`** (0.55). 0x0026B9EC
    lies inside the FACTS-confirmed BattleAi block; the caller
    `BattleAi_SelectActionForCurrentActor` reads `record + slot*0xE + 0xA` as a spell id. The
    0xE-stride record is a 6-slot tactics plan (five u16 condition ids + action byte + flags),
    not recipe ingredients.
  - `maybe_Battle_Monster_CheckRowState` → `maybe_Battle_CheckTargetReceivesAction`
    (action row + unit status ids 0x6F/0x70/0xFF + status immunity + battle-RNG threshold; there
    is no monster or message-row code).
  - `maybe_Battle_RollActionRowSuccess` → `maybe_Battle_RollActionStatusChance`
    (SkillRecord chance + `maybe_Battle_RollStatusProc`).
  - `maybe_Actor_FindNextEntryForCharacter` → `Party_FindNextEntryForCharacter` (all three
    confident callers are `Party_*` and the body walks party character-entry rows).
  - Message-window close pair renamed after what they actually notify:
    `maybe_Ui_MessageWindow_CloseAndReleaseCutscene` / `..._CloseAndNotifyCharacter`
    (the old "WithSpeaker"/"WithEmoteLine" wording was unsupported).
  - `maybe_Gfx_SceneNode_EnsureChild` → `maybe_Ui_StateSequence_LazyCreate` (vtable 0x0075ABA0
    methods are `std_StateSequence67_*`, 11 states, no child-node accesses).
  - Factories renamed after their named constructors: `maybe_Ui_CreateItemMenuWidget`,
    `maybe_Ui_CreateDialogWidget`; constructors renamed after their real shape:
    `maybe_Field_SceneObject_CtorWithChildren`, `maybe_Field_SceneObject_CtorType7`.
  - Semantic fixes: `maybe_Battle_Ui_Step_PostUnitMessage` (no hit effect),
    `maybe_Field_CreateSpriteActor` (not a scene effect), `maybe_Ui_ItemScreen_ShowSelectionResult`
    (posts confirmation messages, never edits the bag), `maybe_MenuActor_ApplyModelOverride`
    (no animation play), `maybe_Battle_PickUnitNearAverageLevel`,
    `maybe_Field_MapJumpJob_Poll`, `maybe_Ui_ListWidget_ShowCurrentRow`.
- **22 kept_maybe** (0.30–0.45), summaries sharpened, no confidence inflation. The two damaged
