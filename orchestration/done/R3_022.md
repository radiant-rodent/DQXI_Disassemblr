### Two evidence corrections that affected the wording of many entries

1. **`func_0010F6CC` is not plain `operator delete`.** Disassembly
   `0x0010F6CC: cmp r0,#0; beq; sub r1,r0,#8; ldrd r0,r1,[r1]; ldr r2,[r0]; ldr r2,[r2,#4]; bx r2`
   loads the two words before the object and dispatches through their vtable+4, i.e. a custom
   allocator/free thunk. The dtor summaries now say "shared free thunk 0x0010F6CC" instead of
   repeating the old "operator delete" claim.
2. **Dossier caller lists contain mis-attributions.** The `bl` target is sometimes mapped to the
   nearest preceding symbol when the real callee has no symbol (or is a mid-function split).
   Examples found here: `maybe_Ui_BigView_Dtor`'s eight model "callers" all call 0x001C9B8C, and
   `Ui_CasinoSlotMetalChange_Dtor`'s four `Fs_*` "callers" do not call it. I used vtable/data
   words and readable bodies as the primary evidence, not those caller lists.
