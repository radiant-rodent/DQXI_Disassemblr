# R5_015 — second naming pass (refine) on 149 low-confidence `maybe_` functions

## What I did

Worked through all 149 addresses of `orchestration/naming/refine/batches/R5_015.txt` using the
pre-baked dossiers (`orchestration/naming/refine/dossiers/R5_015.md`), plus the recovered readable
bodies under `decomp/readable/recovered/` for the 14 entries that have no
`decomp/readable/functions/` file.

Verdicts: **25 confirmed, 11 corrected, 113 kept as `maybe_`** (of which 2 were re-worded).

The dominant finding is that this batch is full of functions whose readable C is *entirely*
destroyed by the `std_ConstructArray` "does not return" decompiler bug: the whole body collapses to
one call and the callee list is polluted by the inlined lazy-initialisation idiom
(`func_0011FE8C` / `func_0011CFAC` / `func_00116AE0` / `std_ConstructArray`) plus a handful of
false callees (`Gfx_Scene_SpriteCtx_Init`, `Os_QueueHasNewDataAlt`, `Container_ResizeArray`,
`Mem_Pool_DestroyAllEntries`). Those callee lists were treated as noise, not evidence. For those
functions the only usable facts were the surviving direct stores, the vtable literal, and the
named callers — so most stayed at `maybe_`.

## Honest caveats / not verified

- **113 functions remain `maybe_`.** For most of them the readable body is a single
  `std_ConstructArray` call; I did not invent names for them. In several cases I could not even
  verify the existing wording (e.g. `maybe_Container_MultiList_Init`, the seven
  `maybe_Ui_Object_DtorStringList*` template copies, `maybe_Ui_WidgetWithTenChildren_Ctor`) —
  those names are layout descriptions, not identified classes.
- **The seven `maybe_Ui_Object_DtorStringList*` functions (0x002E4488, 0x002E4BD4, 0x0046B5C0,
  0x0046BDEC, 0x00591378, 0x00591918, and the non-deleting 0x002E4C20) are one template
  instantiated per container type.** Only the 0x002E4C20 copy had a vtable literal pointing at
  what it is; the rest stay `maybe_` and their A..G suffixes are just disambiguators.
- **Two groups of byte-identical "activate/reset" templates** (0x00195148, 0x002A5B4C, 0x002A5C60,
  0x002A6464, 0x002FD158, 0x002FD918, 0x002FDD50, 0x00307C54, 0x00308110, 0x002A6B08, 0x00308D10)
  and their sibling 0x00307F88 are still class-unknown. The recovered bodies use `self` as an
  undeclared identifier and lose the argument registers, so `unaff_*`/junk tokens appear in them;
  per FACTS those stay at confidence <= 0.3.
- Two names I deliberately left alone rather than guess further: `maybe_Ui_Overlay_InitFromParams`
  (0x00362C04 — its only caller is `Party_InitMemberRoster` and the body is compiler alignment
  arithmetic, so the overlay attribution is probably wrong but nothing better is proven) and
  `maybe_Field_Action_EndCut`/`EndDig` (names inferred only from Start/End pairing).
- `Maybe_Ui_PartyPanelSlot_ResetEntry` was considered for 0x001E3638 but rejected: the only link is
  the `g_uiPartyPanelSlotObjectCallbacks` pivot floats, which is too weak to rename a text-window
  routine.
