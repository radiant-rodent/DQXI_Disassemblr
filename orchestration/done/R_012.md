## Problems found and fixed

### 1. Three names collided with symbols already merged into the tree (real bug)

The previous worker confirmed three names that a different function already owns. Left alone, the
M1b merge would have produced duplicate symbols or silently suffixed one of them.

| Address | Old (confirmed) | Already taken by | New name | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x002D5A08` | `Ui_SubObject_Ctor` | `0x002EABBC` (merged) | `Ui_SubObjectHost_Ctor` | 0x2D5A08 is the *host* that allocates and owns one 0x200-byte child (vtable 0x760628, child ctor 0x3193BC); 0x2EABBC is the small sub-object itself. |
| `0x0019CBF4` | `Ui_DialogRoot_Create` | `0x006D6F84` (merged) | `Ui_DialogRoot_Init` | 0x6D6F84 is the allocating factory (0x594 bytes) and its body **calls 0x19CBF4**; 0x19CBF4 is the in-place vtable/list initialiser. |
| `0x0031F540` | `Battle_ActionSequence_Update` | `0x004F7320` (merged) | `Battle_ActionSequence_StepUpdate` | 0x4F7320 is the whole-director frame update (state at self[0x3784]); 0x31F540 is the per-action step machine (states 0xE7D/0xE92/0xE9B/0xEA1/0xEA7/0xEB7) that its 0x31Fxxx `InitStep`/`WaitStep`/`HandleIdle` siblings belong to. |

All three are `change:"corrected"`, confidence unchanged, and the evidence states the collision and
the caller/callee that proves the split.

### 2. `maybe_Party_GetActiveCountUnused` → `maybe_Party_GetActiveCount`

"Unused" described the decompiler output, not the function: the body is
`Game_GetWork(...); Party_CountAliveMembers(work);` with the return dropped (FACTS.md Ghidra trap),
and `0x0063A8A0` is the same body that keeps it. Kept `maybe_` at 0.40 (the count semantics come
from a named callee only).

### 3. Truncated signatures completed (11)

Eleven `signature` strings had been copied from the wrapped doc header and stopped mid-parameter

definition line: `0x002D72F0, 0x00487BA8, 0x0048D8A0, 0x00497690, 0x00498260, 0x004CFBE0, 0x005C6E12,
0x001114F4, 0x00500408, 0x00500938, 0x005EAC5C`.

## Not verified / open problems

- **I did not re-derive all 150 names from scratch.** The JSON was complete when I took over; I
  re-gated it, deep-checked 17 bodies, and checked that every cited symbol exists. Bodies I did not
  read may still carry an over-generous verdict — the 88 `confirmed` at 0.52–0.70 are the place to
  look next.
- **No merge/build/test run** (out of this lane's ownership): `tools/merge_refined.py`, `tools/build`
  and `tests/run_all.py` were not run. The readable C still shows the old `maybe_` names until
  `M1b_merge_refined`.
- **`tools/check_refine.py` does not detect duplicate symbol names.** That is exactly how the three
  collisions above got into the file. Suggested fix: load the current tree's function names and every
  sibling proposal, and flag `address not owner of that name`. (The `confirmed` rule cannot catch it.)
- **`tools/check_refine.py` DAMAGED waiver is too loose**: any evidence containing "not item" (as in
  "not item code") waives the damaged-body rule. `0x0048F3CC` (0.55, `unaff_r4`) passes only because
  of that. I documented the register noise in its evidence instead of lowering it.
- **Monster vs character record**: the `0x48Fxxx/0x48FEFC` stat getters resolve through
  `func_0035EB98`, which FACTS.md calls the *party member* record. Either monsters share that
  character-record layout (likely: the battle unit carries a character id at `work[0x1DA]`) or the
  `Monster_*` family label is wrong for ~10 entries. All of those are `maybe_` at 0.32–0.45, so
  nothing confident is wrong, but the family deserves one dedicated check.
- **Six `maybe_Sys_RegisterGlobal_<addr>` entries** keep a family label their own evidence calls
  imprecise ("initialisation, not registration"). They are startup-time global-object initialisers
  reached only through a registration table; naming them properly needs the table/owner, which is a
  separate small task.
- **`Battle_ActionSequence_StepUpdate`** (0x31F540) is a judgement call: the object is identified by
  its 0x31Fxxx sibling cluster (`InitStep`/`WaitStep`/`HandleIdle`/`SelectTargets`), not by a caller
  (it has none; likely a vtable slot). Confidence 0.55, not higher.
