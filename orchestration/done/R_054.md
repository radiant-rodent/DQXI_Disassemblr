## Method (what produced the new evidence)

The batch was almost entirely context score 0: no named caller, no named callee (most call targets are
mid-function snippets that have no symbol entry). The decisive evidence therefore came from the **vtable
slot** each function occupies:

1. `out/scratch/R_054/digest2.py` — one dump of all 150 readable files with their headers, resolved
   callees and reverse caller hits.
2. `out/scratch/R_054/vtables.py` — byteswaps the word at every address in `asm/rodata` + `asm/data`,
   resolves each vtable slot's function name from `symbols_recovered.json` and prints the ±4/±8
   neighbouring slots. That turned "the header claims class X" into "row 0x… is in the table whose other
   slots are the confident A, B, C", which is where all 35 confirmations and 3 of the 6 corrections came
   from.
3. `out/scratch/R_054/entries{1,2,3}.py` + `build.py` — the entries written in three parts (a resumed
   worker can pick up one part) and merged into the JSON; `build.py` re-checks coverage, prefix/confidence
   consistency, `change` values and that `previous_name`/`previous_confidence` match the batch.

## Kept `maybe_` (109) — why

- The whole Ui_DateDisplay step family (0x002C1EA8-0x002C2624): the {step,arg} table has **no** confident
  sibling, and the state numbers are only the literals passed to the timer helper.
- ~25 deleting-destructor stubs: the body (`bl base_dtor; b operator_delete`) is a certain *shape*, but
  the base-dtor address is an inner entry of an unnamed function and the class label only comes from
  maybe_ neighbours. `maybe_Field_NpcAction_DeletingDtor` also keeps its maybe_ because a confident
  `Field_NpcAction_DeletingDtor` already exists at 0x00332D90 for a different class.
- The damaged decompilations: 0x002CC614 (`unaff_r5`), 0x002C49AC (`unaff_r4/r7`), 0x002D004C (unaff_,
  mid-Thumb), 0x002F2210 (only flag registers), 0x002EBB2C (a single garbage expression), 0x002F6A64
  (SBORROW4/CARRY4 chains), 0x002C8EA2/0x002D3E06/0x002DF856 (phantom/mid-instruction entries), plus
  `0x002C3244` whose body is truncated right after the inline-storage guard.
- The five identical `Fs_` lookup wrappers (0x2E5270/B/C, 0x2E5A38, 0x2E5DB4, 0x2E6868): the shared
  lookup helpers 0x007141A8/0x00714160 are unnamed, so "resource table" is still a family guess.
- `Ui_ItemInfoWindow_*` at 0x002F6B48/0x002F6B74: the confident `Ui_ItemInfoWindow_Dtor` lives at
  0x0045287C in a different table (0x00771428), so these smaller objects are probably a sibling class;
  the batch's `_2` suffix was kept and the doubt is written into both entries.
