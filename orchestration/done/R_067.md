## What this batch is

The functions in R_067 span 0x0053682C through 0x005C5B08, with the bulk (129 functions) in
`blk:00540000` (0x00540000..0x0057FFFF). This region houses the game's audio middleware (Nintendo CTR
SDK sound, `.bcbnk` sound banks, `.bcwar` wave archives, stream channels, 3D audio listener attenuation),
file streaming and archive management (`Fs_ArchiveStream_*`, `Fs_NamedObject_*`, `Pack_*`), font atlas rasterization,
and the tail of Field2D and Battle2D unit classes.

Because batch N_116 had broadly tagged much of 0x0054xxxx with `Ui_Layout_*`, `Ui_TextRun_*`, `Ui_TextColorRange_*`,
and `Ui_Scene_*`, re-reading this block in context against now-confident neighbours and vtables uncovered a
significant poisoned neighbourhood where sound and audio attenuation routines were misclassified as UI code.
