## Open Problems & Suggested Follow-Up Tasks
- **`struct_db` `int*`-stride virtual call artifacts**: When Ghidra types `self` as `int*` and emits `(**(code **)(*param_1 + 0x1c))(...)`, the struct harvester records a field at `0x1c * 4 = 0x70` (`Gfx_Screen`, `Gfx_AnimSequence`, `Gfx_EffectNodeCreateChild`, `Gfx_ResourceSlot`). Filtering `*param_1 + const` dereferences out of `tools/typedb/struct_db.py` would prevent virtual table offsets from being harvested as struct fields.
- **`Gfx_SceneGroup` vs `Fx_System` merge**: `Gfx_SceneGroup` (`+0x460..+0x4D8`) is the telemetry/channel-mask tail of the `0x538`-byte `Fx_System` manager (`0x0052E9C8`, `0x0052F988`, `0x005304AC`). Once `Fx_System` is unified in `structs.json`, these fields can be merged into the main `Fx_System` struct definition.
