## What the batch turned out to be

The 15 "classes" are Squirrel (script VM) internals, recovered from a GCC/ARM32 build of Squirrel
2.2-style bytecode. Several machine-generated classes mix two different objects or are not classes
at all; those cases are documented in the class `summary`/`evidence` instead of being hidden.

## Decisions (as required by the worker rules)

1. **Kept every generated class name/type** (`Script_Vm`, `Script_Table`, `Script_SqProto`, …) even
   where the evidence shows a different real identity, because readable C and `structs.json` already
   use them in signatures. The real identity and the contamination are stated in `summary`/`evidence`.
2. **Never invented a field purpose.** Offsets whose only evidence is a mis-decoded body (0x34 of
   `Script_NativeTable`, 0x364 of `Script_ResourceManager`, 0x90 of `Script_ByteStream`) are
   `unknown_*` at confidence 0.0 with an explanation of the extraction artefact
   (pointer arithmetic / vtable-slot word index multiplied by 4). The nine zero-initialised words
   inside the UI object's +0x34 descriptor block are `unknown_gapXX` 0.0.
3. **`Script_Vm` is named as `SQSharedState`.** The batch size (0xB8) and the fields from
   InitTables/ReleaseTables/RegisterDefaultDelegates all belong to the shared state; the 0xA8-byte
   SQVM whose `_top`/`_stackbase` are at +0x30/+0x34 was merged into the same class by struct_db.
   The class summary says so and +0x30/+0x34 are named `registryTag`/`registryValue` per the
   constructor evidence; `Script_Table` (same offsets, VM API) is named `stackTop`/`stackBase`.
4. **Sizes 48 → 52** for `Script_SqValueList` and `Script_SqFuncState`: the inline `SQObjectPtr` at
   +0x2C is 8 bytes, so the batch's `max_strong_field+width` undercounted by 4. `apply.py` recomputes
   the size from the fields anyway; the change is documented in the class summary.
5. **8-byte SQObjectPtr pairs are emitted as `xxxTag` (u32) + `xxxValue` (pointer/void*)** because
   the dispatcher requires every 4-byte batch offset exactly once and no overlapping members.
6. Field widths were corrected where the batch's default 4 would overlap the next member
   (`u8` fields at 0x58/0x59, and 0x20/0x21 in `Script_SqProto`).

## Open problems / not verified

- The three intrusive list heads of `Script_UiSystemObject` (+0x04…+0x28) are initialised but never
  used by the two ctors; their node layout could not be recovered. They are `maybe_list*` at 0.3.
- `Script_UiSystemObject` +0x34…+0x60 (the block whose first word is 0x00782534) has no reader in
  the batch; named `maybe_typeMapVtable`/`maybe_nameMapVtable` + `unknown_gap*`.
- `Script_Object` +0x0C is only passed by address to 0x007133F0; its true type is unknown.
- The `Script_Vm` tail field order (`compilerErrorHandler` 0xA4, print func 0xA8, two bools, then
  scratchpad) follows upstream Squirrel; only the scratchpad (freed with its size) and the error
  handler (called from Compile) are directly proven.
- `Script_ResourceManager` +0x0 is not proven to be a vtable (0.35).
- The identity of the holder at `Script_SqValueList` +0x1C (count at +0x24, element base at +0x38)
  is not resolved; only its release path is proven.
