## Important honesty notes (misattributions in the batch)

`struct_db.py` typed some caller arguments as `self`, so several fields belong to a different object:

* `Script_Native_ATrgMapJumpEvent` (0x00251230) operates on **EventState**: trigger records of
  0x18 bytes at +0x98..+0x9C, loop index +0x6C, callable +0x70, abort byte +0x3D8, script context
  +0x3DC. The fields are named by their observed role and the evidence says so.
* `Script_Array_Reverse` (0x006BA944) and `Script_Closure_BindEnv` (0x006BAA68) operate on the
  **Script VM**: +0x30 stack top, +0x34 stack base.
* `Sq_Array_Create` (0x005F9D8C) registers the new array in the **VM** list at vm+0x48; that field is
  emitted as `maybe_vmObjectList` (0.35), not as an array member.
* `Sq_Container` has only its destructor in the evidence. The five inline 8-byte value slots
  (+0x38..+0x58) and the role of the second 8-byte array (+0x24) cannot be identified, so they are
  `maybe_valueSlot0..4` (0.30) and `maybe_secondaryValueArray` (0.45) rather than invented roles.
* `Script_TCSJump` +0x14C is the inline `ModelAnimInstance` passed to the `Anim_ModelInstance_Release`
  split at 0x005AD368 — named `maybe_modelAnimInstance` (0.40) because only 4 bytes of it are in
  this batch.

No `unknown_*` at confidence 0.0 was needed: every offset has at least a structural role.
