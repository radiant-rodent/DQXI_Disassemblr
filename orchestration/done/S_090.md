## Decisions worth knowing

1. **struct_db offset aliasing.** Several fields are projected onto the wrong base because a constructor advanced its pointer first:
   - `Ui_PagedListWindow` +0x18/+0x1C are aliases of the real +0x6C/+0x70 (the ctor does `puVar2 = func_00622248(puVar2 + 0x15)` before `puVar2[6]/[7]`). The true owner is named at +0x70; the aliases are `maybe_rowCapacity` / `maybe_ownerWindowMirror` at 0.35/0.2.
   - `Ui_DungeonMessageWindow` +0x00/+0x04/+0x08 come from `BindTextPanes(param_1 = &window->f_11c)`, i.e. they are +0x11C/+0x120/+0x124. Kept as low-confidence aliases; the real fields are named at those offsets.
   - `Ui_DungeonListWindow` +0x20 is the member-pair owner store at +0x140.
2. **Virtual-call artefacts.** `(**(code **)(*self + 0xNN))(...)` produced fake fields. They are emitted as `unknown_0xNN` with confidence 0.0 and the real vtable slot in the evidence, not invented purposes.
3. **`Ui_DiveResultWindow` class name.** The only recovered method (0x0032C33C) is documented as `maybe_Ui_DiveManager_Update` with vtable 0x007641B8 labelled `Ui_DiveManager`. The batch/dispatcher name is kept (rename would break signatures), but the summary and class confidence (0.45) say the newer evidence points at `Ui_DiveManager`.
4. **Intrusive list nodes** at +0x11C/+0x150/+0x180/+0x1B0 etc. are named `maybe_listNode`/`maybe_*Head` because 0x00123594 and 0x001235A4 have no readable C — only disassembly (0x00123594..0x001235C4 stores next/prev pointers) shows linked-list semantics.
