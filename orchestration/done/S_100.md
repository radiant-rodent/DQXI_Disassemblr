## What was written

15 classes: `Ui_InnMessageWindow`, `Ui_KeyAnimWindow`, `Ui_LoadingWindow`, `Ui_Message3DWindow`,
`Ui_PartyWindow`, `Ui_RecipeInfoWindow`, `Ui_SlotArrayWindow`, `Ui_SoulSelectWindow`,
`Ui_TextScrollWindow`, `Ui_ThreeLayoutWindow`, `Ui_ThreePairNumberWindow`,
`Ui_ThreeRowNumberedTextWindow`, `Ui_TitleListWindow`, `Ui_TitleNameWindow`,
`Ui_TwoAnimListWindow`.

Name mix: **20 clear names, 3 `maybe_*`, 7 `unknown_*` at confidence 0.0**. Examples of the
stronger results:

* `Ui_KeyAnimWindow` +0x50 / +0xA0 = `keyAnim` / `keyLoopAnim` (strings `key_anim` / `key_loop`,
  `Ui_AnimRef_Bind` calls).
* `Ui_LoadingWindow` +0x4C = `loadingAnim`, +0x138 = `paneAnimList` (the +0x138 object is the
  receiver of `Ui_Widget_FindPaneAndNotify` and is switched visible by `Ui_AnimRefList_SetVisible`).
* `Ui_InnMessageWindow` +0xC = `animRefList` (`Ui_AnimRefList*`; vtable slot +0x18 is
  `Ui_AnimRefList_SetVisible`).
* `Ui_TextScrollWindow` is really a **scrollbar**: vtable `0x0075EA1C` slot 1 is
  `Ui_ScrollBar_Update`; +8 = `listController` (documented as the list the scrollbar mirrors).
* `Ui_SlotArrayWindow` +0x54 = inline `u8[0x5C0]` `slotArray` (16 × 0x5C objects destroyed by
  `std_destroy_range`; vtable slot 8 is `Ui_SixteenRowCheckListWindow_InitPanes`).
* `Ui_SoulSelectWindow` +0x28 / +0x2C = `sourceWindow` / `childWindow` (child lazily created from
  `Ui_ItemInfoWindow_Create`; visibility copied from source +0x94).

Decisions (also in `AGENT_LOG.md`):

* The batch offsets `0xD0`, `0xE0`, `0x120` in six classes are **struct_db artefacts**: they are
  `(**(code **)(*self + 0x34/0x38/0x48))(...)` vtable slot bytes multiplied by 4 for an `int*`
  self. They are emitted as `unknown_vtableSlot13/14/18` at 0.0 with the real slot named.
* 0x50-byte animation units are typed `Ui_AnimRefList` (80 bytes), not the 800-byte
  `Ui_AnimRef` artefact.
* Sizes were extended only where embedded extents prove it: KeyAnimWindow 240, LoadingWindow 392,
  RecipeInfoWindow 796, SlotArrayWindow 1556, ThreeLayoutWindow 316, TwoAnimListWindow 236.
  Other sizes are echoed from the batch (they are lower bounds).
* `Ui_TitleListWindow` grouping is doubtful: its two recovered "dtor" bodies release an embedded
  object whose base vtables are `Field_BattleTrigger` (`0x0077627C`) and `Field_Object2D`
  (`0x0077D8BC`). Field +0x3C is therefore `unknown_subObject` at 0.0 and the class confidence is
  0.2.
