## Decisions and problems found

1. **struct_db scales some offsets by 4.** `param_1[i]` dword indexing and vtable-slot
   expressions were multiplied by the element width, so e.g. Ui_EquipPanel 0x30..0x70 are
   really 0x0C..0x1C, Ui_StatusPanel 0x4C/0x50/0x54 are really 0x13/0x14/0x15,
   Ui_RankingPanel 0xFE0 is really 0x3F8, Ui_Panel 0x88/0x228/0x22C are really
   0x22/0x8A/0x8B. I named the *real* member and wrote the batch→real conversion into the
   field evidence rather than pretending the batch offset is literal. Pure virtual-call
   artefacts (Ui_SkillPanel 0xD0/0xE0/0x1F0, Ui_PartyPanel 0x100/0x140/0x220) are
   `unknown_*` at 0.0.
2. **Ui_CharacterPanel is based at +0x1F4 in the batch.** The ctor's `piVar5` is the
   secondary base (primary vtable 0x0075C5CC at real base 0, secondary vptr at +0x1F4), so
   the batch's `size: 256` only covers real +0x1F4..+0x2F0 of a 0x2F4-byte object. I kept the
   batch size so the merger does not reject offsets, but this should be fixed upstream.
3. **Width corrections** where the batch's dword width came from that scaling bug:
   Ui_SkillPanel entryIdTable u16, Ui_EquipPanel slots u16 / category u8, Ui_RankingPanel
   slot bytes u8, Ui_StatusPanel layoutMode u8, Ui_PartyPanel waitCounter s8 (sizes set to
   the true width).
4. The four `weak` Ui_Panel fields at 0x48B4..0x48B8 are far outside the 13688-byte class
   and are emitted as `unknown_*` 0.0.
5. Nothing was invented to fill an offset: 4 fields are `unknown_*` 0.0, ~110 are `maybe_*`
   at 0.15–0.45. The Ui_Panel 16 reset words and Ui_CharacterPanel colour pairs are
   structural names only.
