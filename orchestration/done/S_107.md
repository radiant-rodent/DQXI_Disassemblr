## Decisions

1. **Kept every generated class name and `type`** (the merger keys on them). Stale names are flagged in
   the summary/evidence, never silently renamed.
2. **Sizes were corrected where the allocation/array extent is proven**, because the batch's
   `max_strong_field+4` is a lower bound and several classes embed trailing objects:
   MemberRowWidget 624 (0x270 allocation), FourStateWidget 396 (4×0x50 at +0x4C),
   ChildWidget 1780 (0x204 + 4×0x13C), DamagePopupWidget 2996 (0x1DC + 15×0xA8),
   CursorWidget 156 (0x4C + 0x50 anim ref), TreeWidget 392 (0x138 + 0x50 Widget5Anim),
   TextArgWidget 18264 (last write at +0x4754). `Ui_TextWidget` was kept at the batch's 356 because the
   only higher offset is a vtable-slot artifact (real minimum 140 = 0x4C + `Ui_LayoutRef`).
3. **Vtable-slot artifacts are `unknown_*` at confidence 0.0**, matching S_092/S_093/S_096/S_098/S_101:
   offsets 0xD0/0xE0/0x120 (`*self+0x34/0x38/0x48` × 4) in DigitRow/FourRow/SixRow,
   0x60 (`+0x18`) in CursorWidget, 0x160 (`+0x58`) in TextWidget, 0x170 (`+0x5C`) in ItemListWidget.
   Also parser artifacts: Ui_MemberRowWidget +0x250 (`*storage+0x94`), the Ui_TreeWidget +0x4C store
   (it goes to `child+0x4C` = base+0x238).
4. **The shared +0x11C 8-word block** in MemberRowWidget/TreeWidget is an embedded UI child-list object
   (literal vtable 0x00759388, two self-linked ring heads). It is described structurally
   (`maybe_embeddedVtable`, `maybe_childListBegin/End/Capacity`, `maybe_nodeRingHead/Prev/Next`, 0.3-0.4);
   no stronger meaning is claimed.
5. **`Ui_TextArgWidget` / `Ui_ColorVariantWidget` are not constructors** despite the roles in the batch:
   0x0025D760/0x0025DE10/0x0025DFC4/0x0025E0BC contain no vtable store. They are methods of large
   menu screens, and the fields are named from their call sites (`maybe_modeState`,
   `maybe_elementGroup`, `rowList`, `maybe_messageArgValue`, `maybe_stateWidget`).
6. The `Ui_TextWidget` batch merges two unrelated classes (registry-node ctor 0x002A55EC and gauge-window
   dtor 0x003991FC). Field offsets are named from whichever body proves them; the class-level confidence
   is 0.3 and the summary says so.

## Open problems / not verified

* Every `maybe_*` and `unknown_*` field (13 unknown at 0.0, 20 maybe_): the exact container semantics of
  the +0x11C block, the argument meanings of IconWidget +0xC..+0x1C, the message-arg trio at
  TextArgWidget +0x940..+0x948 and the variant lookup inputs at ColorVariantWidget +0x720/+0x984.
* `Ui_TextArgWidget` size 18264 is a lower bound; the embedded four-state widget may extend it to ~0x48E0.
  `Ui_ColorVariantWidget` 5548 is likewise a lower bound before its +0x15A8 widget.
* `Ui_TextWidget` real extent (~0x8C) versus batch 356; `Ui_ItemListWidget`/the three text-row windows
  sizes are still artifact-derived (no allocation evidence was recovered).
* `Ui_CursorWidget` base `Ui_AnimRef` is questionable: the ctor initialises its own animation at +0x4C and
  fields at +0xC/+0x10/+0x24/+0x28 that do not fit the known AnimRef layout. The batch base was kept.
* Class attribution of `Ui_TextArgWidget` and `Ui_ColorVariantWidget` (two methods each, no caller) is weak;
  they may belong to one or more larger menu-screen classes.
