## Decisions (some contradict the machine-generated batch)

1. **Kept every generated `class` and `type`** (the merger keys on `class`). Wrong identities are
   stated in `summary`/`evidence` instead of renaming:
   * `Ui_ActorWidget` — its two "dtors" (0x004A37B8 / 0x004A37E8) are a generic intrusive-list
     teardown pair; the deleting one is slot 0 of vtable 0x00775534, which belongs to
     `Field_HandleRef`. Nothing in the bodies names a widget.
   * `Ui_SixPaneWidget` — its two "ctors" are the `Ui_LoadWindow` destructor pair
     (0x002A504C / 0x002A5000).
2. **16 of the 69 offsets are struct_db vtable-slot artefacts.** `(**(code**)(*param_1 + N))` was
   scraped as a field at `N*4`: `Ui_TenRowTextWidget` 0xD0/0xE0/0x120, `Ui_RowWidget` 0xD0/0xE0,
   `Ui_SixLineTextWidget` 0xE0/0x120, `Ui_NumberTextWidget` 0xD0, `Ui_TextNumberWidget` 0xD0,
   `Ui_CommandBar` 0x60/0xD0/0x100, `Ui_ScrollBar` 0xD0/0x180/0x1B0/0x1C0/0x1D0. They are emitted as
   `unknown_<hex>` at confidence 0.0 with the real expression quoted — never as a guessed field.
   Consequence: the batch sizes of `Ui_TenRowTextWidget` (292), `Ui_RowWidget` (228),
   `Ui_SixLineTextWidget` (292), `Ui_NumberTextWidget` (212), `Ui_TextNumberWidget` (212) and
   `Ui_CommandBar` (260) are **lower bounds only**, because they are "highest artefact + 4".
3. **`Ui_ScrollBar` 0x79 and 0x88 are interior addresses of the row-id array** (the two sort-loop
   start points in `BuildRows` 0x004D0380), emitted as `unknown_*` 0.0. +0x78 is declared
   `u8 rowIds[100]` (InitFields 0x004D08CC writes 100 bytes of 0xFF there), so the emitter skips
   the two overlapping offsets with a comment.
4. **Sizes corrected where the evidence beats "highest field + 4"**:
   * `Ui_SpriteWidget` 668 → **1252**: the only caller `Ui_Overlay_CreateSlotWidget` (0x00237ADC,
     case 2) allocates `0x4E4` bytes; the ctor's `std_ConstructArray(+0x298, 0x004AF3F4, 0x48, 8)`
     fills +0x298..+0x4D8.
   * `Ui_SixPaneWidget` 488 → **564**: dtor asm `str r1,[r0],#0x1e4` then five `sub r0,#0x50` and
     six `Ui_LayoutRef_Release` calls put the panes at +0x54,+0xA4,+0xF4,+0x144,+0x194,+0x1E4, so
     the object is 0x234 bytes.
5. **`Ui_PartyHpGauge` member stride resolved.** The update loop's `self + i*0x260` is
   member-relative: `Ui_PartyHpGaugeWindow`'s ctor builds four 0x260-byte records at +0x138
   (`FUN_00100BE0(param_1+0x4e, 0x260, 4)`), so the loop's +0x14C / +0x138 / +0x394 are
   member+0x14 (enable byte) / member+0 (the `Battle_HpGaugeWindow`) / member+0x25C (counter), and
   +0xAB8 is the window's frame counter right after the array (0x138 + 4*0x260 = 0xAB8).
6. **`Ui_GaugeBar` +0x58** is the literal `0x42B40000` = **90.0f** (read out of
   `original/exefs/code.decompressed.bin` at 0x003A5548), stored right after the `gauge_bar`
   anim-ref list and never read in the recovered set → `maybe_barLength` at 0.3, not a confident name.
7. **`Ui_Gauge` +0x4C is the maximum, not an animation handle.** `0x002B1E44` clamps the new value
   into `[1, self->f_4c]`; `0x0045EB08`'s `func_00649BD4(&self->f_4c)` is a getter returning
   `(float)*(u32*)(&f_4c + 8)`, i.e. it reads +0x54, not +0x4C.
8. Nothing was invented: every name above 0.5 rests on a string literal (`txt_num`, `gauge_bar_f`,
   `icon_gauge2`, `icon_ex0`), a named callee (`Ui_TextLayer_SetText`, `Ui_LayoutRef_Release`,
   `Item_GetStoragePointer`, `Battle_HpGaugeWindow_Update`, `Ui_PartySelectWindow_BindDataSource`),
   a vtable slot or a direct store/compare. 16 fields are `unknown_` at 0.0, 12 are `maybe_*` < 0.5.

## Open problems / suggested follow-ups

* `tools/typedb/struct_db.py` should not turn `(**(code**)(*obj + N))` into a field at `N*4`. That
  single fix would remove 16 of the 69 offsets in this batch and stop the derived sizes of the six
  small widgets from being artefacts.
* `Ui_ActorWidget` and `Ui_SixPaneWidget` are mis-clustered: the first is a generic intrusive-list
  owner (its deleting dtor is `Field_HandleRef`'s), the second is `Ui_LoadWindow`'s destructor pair.
  Both should be re-clustered by vtable in `struct_db.py`/`make_batches.py`.
* `Ui_ScrollBar` merges two objects (outer part vtable 0x0075EA1C, inner list vtable 0x0076120C).
  Splitting the cluster by vtable would let +0x04/+0x08/+0x20/+0x22..0x24/+0x28 be named properly.
* `Ui_ScrollBar` +0x208 points at a record whose +0x768 is a party-member index; that record class
  is not identified (candidate: a party-member row of the strength/sakusen window).
* `Ui_TwoPaneWidget` has exactly one recovered method; a follow-up should look for its ctor and
  pane-binding init so the 0x25EC visibility group gets siblings.
* `Ui_Gauge` +0xAC/+0xB0/+0xB4 are only known through arithmetic (a divisor and a range); a caller
  of 0x0045EB08 would settle them — it currently has none.
