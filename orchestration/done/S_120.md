## Open problems / not verified

* Every `maybe_*` / `unknown_*` field (about a third of the batch) is hypothesis-level.
* `struct_db` merged several objects into `Ui_JackpotScreen` (screen + its animation object +
  `Ui_RouletteWindow` member) and into `Ui_Overlay` (a small screen-flash object). The class keys
  were kept because the dispatcher requires the generated name; the caveats are in the class
  `evidence`.
* `Ui_JackpotScreen` +0xC: `SetFadeColors` proves it is a 0..1 fade value, while the ctor writes
  `0xFFFFF000` (−1.0 in 20.12 fixed point) — the two functions may be reading two different
  objects at the same offset. Named `fadeValue` on the method evidence.
* 14 of the `Ui_JackpotScreen` +0x220..+0x2E8 "anim" words only have default-value stores, so
  their names are placeholders (`maybe_animFieldNNN`).
* Six `Ui_IconCache` offsets +0x80..+0xD0 are vtable-slot artefacts, not real fields
  (`unknown_*` at 0.0).
* Sub-window class identities at `Ui_MessagePage` +0x188..+0x2D4 are inferred from vtable calls,
  not from allocation sites.
