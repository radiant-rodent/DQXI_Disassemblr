## Left out

None. Every variable in every listing was named.

Already-named entities were not renamed: the first-pass names on each function's "already used names" note (self,
i/j/k, f14, fStack_*, the segment/layout locals, cStack_* members of Ui_ItemListWindow_InitRows, etc.) were kept as
they were.

Uncontestable but weak evidence was labelled rather than dropped: the decompiler register artifacts
(SUB41/FPSRC/CR/S-register temporaries) in Gfx_TrailMesh_BuildSorted, maybe_Effect_CreateEightEmitters and
Anim_Clip_Start/SpawnAndApply carry generic names (fpStatus, partialByte, zeroValue, scratchReg, …), and the inline
ASCII pane-name stack groups in Ui_ItemInfoWindow_InitPanes / Ui_ItemListWindow_InitRows are named positionally
(groupNPartM, iconNameNPartM) because the individual string contents were not visible in the listings.
