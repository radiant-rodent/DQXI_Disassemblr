## Recurring shapes named the same way

- Anonymous draw/setup descriptor blocks (0x005C8738, 0x005CCF88, 0x005E6048, 0x005F6E50, 0x005D19B8):
  0xFFFF `*_idSentinel`, 0x10 `*_textureFilterA/B`, 0xe `*_primitiveType`, 1.0f `*_scaleX/Y/Z`,
  0.0f `*_rotationAngle`, 0.3f `*_alphaValue`, 100.0f `*_depthValue`, 200x120 `*_quadWidth/Height`.
- "alloc, build with ctor, register in list + handle map" pattern: `allocPtr`/`tempObject`, `meshParamsPtr`,
  `allocatorHandle`/`heapTagCopy`, `handleEntry`/`foundObject`.
- Copy-of-parameter stack slots: `heapCopy`/`playerCopy`/`memberIdCopy`, `selfCopy`/`resCopy`/`isAltCopy`,
  `partIdCopy`/`flagsCopy`.
- Temp registers that change meaning mid-function (also present in the first pass' leftovers):
  `tempValue`, `tempInt`, `tempObject`, `tempResult`.

## What was left out and why

- Nothing: no variable of any listed function was left unnamed.
- Four parameters are never read inside their bodies; they were still named for honesty, as `unusedArgN` /
  `*_unusedFrameArg` (0x00617844 arg3/arg4, 0x0065F5B4 arg2/arg3, 0x00660CC0 arg2, 0x0014C728 arg2).
- Deliberately vague but true names were used where the body gives no semantic hook: `zeroValue`,
  `tempValue`, `reservedA/B/C`, `*_flagNN`, `*_scratchWordN`, `*_glyphWordN`, `*_nameWordN`, `wordN`.
- 24 of the 91 entries are below 0.5 confidence (the `maybe_` entries). These are, in order of worse evidence:
  0x00623178 (unassigned registers, wrong header comment), 0x005E54B8 (no dossier, damaged stack record),
  0x0061D65C (dead-code warnings, only structural roles readable), 0x006F333C, 0x00707A7C, 0x0071371C,
  0x00120464, 0x00658D18, 0x006222D0, 0x005CD4C0, 0x005CD576, and the descriptor/flag-only entries
  listed above. They are named from the body, the hint/calls/fields columns and the referenced globals, not
  from documentation.
