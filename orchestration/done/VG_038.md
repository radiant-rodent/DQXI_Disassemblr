## What was left out and why

Nothing on the listed tables was skipped. Two classes deserve comment:

- Unused parameters (`arg2`/`arg3`/`arg8` of several UI/NPC/battle wrappers) are named `unusedArg` / `argValue8`, because the body never reads them and no callee forwards them; the true semantic name cannot be recovered from the caller side without reading callers.
- Very large descriptor builders (0x00336BE4, 0x00337030, 0x0032DE54, 0x0032AC88, 0x0032D300/0x0032D5E8) were named by role + stack offset (`descFlagXX`, `spawnDescXX`, `nameWordN`) instead of inventing field semantics; each entry carries confidence 0.50/0.55 accordingly.
