## What the names describe

Recurring patterns that decided most names:

- **Soft-float compare idioms** (`(uVar >> 0x18)` flag bytes, `SBORROW4`, `SUB41`): named
  `borrowFlag`, `zeroFactor`, `negFlag`, `geFlag`, `nanFlag`, `cmpResult`, `cmpBitsA/B`.
  The pure `(x >> 0x16) & 3` argument of `VectorSignedToFloat` / `VectorUnsignedToFloat` is
  `fpscrValue` (9 functions).
- **Game-work handle chains** (`self->f_XX`, `+0x4254`, `+0x6c`): the reused register is
  `gameWork`-like (`tmpValue`, `workValue`, `scratchValue`), and the occupancy/flag results are
  `stateFlag`, `visibilityFlag`, `isLiveUnit`.
- **Effect spawn descriptors** (`func_0032F98C` + `Effect_FindOrCreateInstance`): the stack
  buffer is `transformDesc`/`effectSpawnParams`, the copied name words are `nameCopy0..11`,
  the copied vector words are `oneVec0..3`, and the vtable slot word is `entryVtable`.
- **Widget/pane lookup loops**: `paneHandle`, `paneNameTable`, `widgetPtr`, `paneDesc`,
  `nodeBase`, `rowEntry`, `wordCursor`.
- **Poker deal variants**: the CONCAT22 card key is `packedCard*`, the saved pair is
  `savedCard*`, the ARM divide leftovers are `remainder*`/`remainingR1*`, the hand slot walker is
  `handSlotPtr`, the deck scan byte is `deckRankByte`.
- **Unused parameters**: `unusedArg`, `unusedArg2`, `unusedArg3`, `unusedArgA` (17 functions).
