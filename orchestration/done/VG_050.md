## Conventions used

- Unread incoming parameters of thunks/handlers are `maybe_scratchArg2`/`maybe_scratchArg3`; the register that is only copied into the game-work out-buffer is named `maybe_gameWorkOutBuf` (params) or `maybe_workOutSeed` (the unused `in_r3` register).
- Repeated zero-initialised stack words that clear a record tail are `maybe_zeroBlock1..16`; repeated copies of `g_vector3Zero` are `maybe_fillVec<N>X/Y/Z`.
- Decompiler-only float-compare carriers keep a descriptive name (`maybe_fpscr`, `maybe_compareFlags`, `maybe_condBits`).
