## Confidence / naming notes

- 41 entries were answered with confidence < 0.5 and therefore have every name prefixed `maybe_`
  (e.g. 0x005988A8, 0x0059D638, 0x005ADC54, 0x005AE414, 0x005B2724).
- Unused parameters are named `maybe_unusedArgN` (only where the body truly never reads them);
  forwarded parameters got the callee's role name (`maybe_updateArg2`, `maybe_playParam`, `maybe_varargs`).
- Stack-buffer families (row/message descriptors in the BuildRowText* and RowPanel* functions, the
  Squirrel SQObjectPtr pairs in the Sq_* functions, the matrix temporaries in the sprite transform
  builders) are named after their slot offset (`rowParam0c`, `maybe_tmp90`, `maybe_mtxb0`) because
  only their position and constant fill were readable, not an individual field meaning.
- In the damaged bodies (0x0059D638, 0x005ADC54, 0x005AE414, 0x005B4CA4) register-resident values
  that are never written are named `maybe_regXX` / `maybe_unusedValue` rather than invented semantics.
