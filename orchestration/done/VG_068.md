## Naming patterns used

- Reused decompiler temporaries (`iVar10`, `uVar4`, `fVar8`, `local_44`) were named for their dominant
  role (`tmpValue`, `tmpWord`, `tmpFloat`, `entryPtr`) rather than for one of their several assignments.
- Stack buffers whose address is passed to a call were named after the callee's purpose
  (`texDescBuf`, `cmdNodeBuf`, `quatBuf`, `outMatrix`, `entryPtr`).
- Descriptor byte/word groups that are filled with a fixed pattern before an emit were named
  positionally (`blendDesc0..4`, `texUnit0..7`, `nodeByte0..15`, `descWord0..5`, `descByte0..7`).
- Saved incoming arguments at the top of a frame were named `<arg>Arg` (`selfArg`, `cmdArg`, `ctxArg`,
  `setArg`, `allocArg`, `resArg`, `handleArg`).
- Unused parameters were named `unusedArg` / `unusedArg2` (verified never read in the body).
- Matrix element copies were named `matrixWord1..6` / `decompWord1..6` / `mat00..mat22`.

## Left out and why

- **Gfx_Sprite_BindResource 0x00587468** — the tool's printed variable table was cut off by its output
  limit, so the entry was accepted as "reviewed with nothing to add". The names visible in the body
  (`iVar1`, `uVar4`, `iVar5`, `uVar8..uVar15`) were not in the printed table and were therefore not
  renamed, per rule 5.
- No other variable was left unnamed. Where the body gave no direct evidence (no readable C, no hint),
  the variable still received a positional but true name and the whole entry was marked below 0.5 with
  the `maybe_` prefix: **maybe_Gfx_ResourcePool_Purge** (0.45), **maybe_Gfx_SpriteSet_InitBaseFromBank**
  (0.45), **maybe_Gfx_CmdBuf_WriteColorCommands** (0.45).
- No name from a function's "already used names" note was reused, and no two variables of one function
  share a name.
