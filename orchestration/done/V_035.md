# V_035 - local variable naming

Batch: `orchestration/types/batches/V_035.json` (150 functions, mostly the sound/mixer and
CECD/icon-atlas areas around 0x0054xxxx-0x006Exxxx).

## What was done

All 150 functions were reviewed through `tools/batchview.py V_035 next` and answered in
`orchestration/types/out/V_035.json`. `python3 tools/batchview.py V_035 --check` reports
`150 answers, 0 problem(s)`; the file parses as JSON (150 records).

- 126 functions received at least one name.
- 527 names total: 15 `argN` parameters and 512 locals.
- 3 names are `maybe_`-prefixed (confidence < 0.5): functions 0x00549288, 0x005543B0,
  0x00556E68.

Naming was driven by the merged field/global names in the readable C, by callee names
(`func_00550B78` -> `mixer`, `func_0054D868` -> `driver`, `func_00547FB0` -> `engine`,
`func_0054EF90` -> `voicePool`, `func_00555B9C` -> `driverQueue`, `func_0011B7E8` ->
`soundThread`), and by the recurring structures in this area: voice/channel/note walks,
member-function-pointer broadcasts (`channel`, `method`, `methodOffset`), and the CECD
icon-atlas state flags.

## What was skipped and why

24 functions were left unnamed (recorded as "reviewed, nothing clear"):

0x005498FC, 0x0054B70C, 0x0054D834, 0x0054DE68, 0x0054DED0, 0x0054DF04, 0x0054DF34,
0x0054F278, 0x005504D0, 0x00550744, 0x00550EA4, 0x00555B60, 0x005561BC, 0x005565F8,
0x00556648, 0x0056ED64, 0x0056F1C4, 0x00655758, 0x006589A8, 0x006626D8, 0x006B948C,
0x006B9518, 0x006E75CC.

Reason in every case: the body is a thin thunk, a pure register shuffle, or a call sequence
with no field access, string, or constant that identifies what the values hold, so any name
would have been a guess. Per the brief, leaving them out was preferred over a wrong name.
