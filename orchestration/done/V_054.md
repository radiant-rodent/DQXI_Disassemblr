# V_054 — local-variable naming batch

## What I did

Worked through all 150 functions of `orchestration/types/batches/V_054.json` with
`python tools/batchview.py V_054 next`, answering each printed chunk with `--add` until the tool
reported "nothing left to name", then ran `--check` (150 answers, 0 problems) and a JSON syntax
check (`json.load` on `orchestration/types/out/V_054.json`, 150 records).

- 150 functions covered, 129 got at least one name, 21 were reviewed and left unnamed.
- 587 local names and 3 parameter renames (`arg4`/`arg5` in `maybe_Ui_Window_SetupChild` →
  `sharedCtx`/`slotIndex`; `list` in `maybe_Ui_Object_DtorWithChild` → `vtable`).
- Every name is lowerCamelCase, no keywords or duplicates, and uses only tokens present in the batch.
  No name was written below confidence 0.5, so no `maybe_`-prefixed names were emitted.
- Naming followed the merged readable C: e.g. `f_26c` → `modelScale` (`Field_HorseAction_BeginJump`),
  `iVar2` → `actor` in `maybe_Field_ActorAction_CheckArrived` (self[10] is the actor stored by the
  base ctor), `f_8b4`/`f_8b0` → `rectCount`/`rectArray` in `Ui_Sprite_HitTest`, and the repeated
  `func_0062F680` results became `hasChildFired` across the `Ui_Window_NotifyHandler*` family.

## What I skipped and why

21 functions had no variable whose role could be justified from the body alone, so they were
recorded as "reviewed, nothing clear" rather than guessed:

- Trivial wrappers/dtors with only `self`/unused args: `Field_HorseAction_BeginLand`,
  `Ui_Object_DtorSimple`, `Ui_Object_DtorSimpleB`, `Ui_Object_DtorSimpleC`,
  `Ui_Row_SetDefaultIconSet`, `Ui_TalkWindow_InitPanes`, `Field_Actor_ResetStep`,
  `maybe_Ui_GetDefaultValue` (only an already-named `result`).
- One-shot vtable+0x40 show/hide helpers (`maybe_Model_Actor_ShowAttachment`,
  `maybe_Model_Actor_HideAttachment`, `maybe_Ui_Window_OpenSlot`, `maybe_Ui_Window_OpenSlotB`,
  `maybe_Ui_Window_CloseSlotB`, `maybe_Ui_Window_CloseSlotC`, `Model_Actor_ShowAttachSlots`,
  `Model_Actor_HideAttachSlots`, `Model_Actor_PlayGripAnimation`,
  `maybe_Model_Actor_FinalizeAttachment`).
- `maybe_Ui_CompositeWindow_CtorLarge` (large multi-vtable dtor): the u8*/int temporaries are
  anonymous vtable/thunk pointers and moving self-offsets, with no defensible nouns.
- `Ui_Object_BroadcastChildrenEvent` and `maybe_Gfx_Actor_ResetComponent`: only a varargs token and
  scratch out-slots remain.

In larger functions I deliberately left the pure type-punned CONCAT/quaternion scratch temporaries
unnamed when their meaning could not be distinguished, and kept multi-purpose reused tokens (e.g.
`iVar1` used first as a bool then as a vtable result) unnamed rather than give them a half-wrong name.

## Notes / decisions

- Reused tokens that hold two different meanings in one body were generally left alone; only tokens
  with one consistent role were renamed.
- Where the record table and the readable C differed (e.g. fields already renamed to
  `targetActor`/`modelActor` by the merge), I renamed only the tokens the batch actually offered and
  treated the field names as evidence.
- Report written with one `AGENT_LOG.md` line appended by shell as instructed.
