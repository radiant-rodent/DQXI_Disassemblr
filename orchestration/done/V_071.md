  still listed as `argN`/stale token, the parameter was renamed to that same
  noun only when the body justified it (`b` → `rightValue` in
  `Sq_VmObjCmpError`, `arg2` → `object`/`owner`/`destSlot`/`vtable` elsewhere).

  `thread/parent/stackSize`) because the readable C and the body both show the
  thread-init semantics.
