  named `*Desc` / `*Fn` / `*Copy` (e.g. `motionDesc`/`motionFn`/`selfCopy`).

- Default-confidence choice: everything is at confidence 0.4-0.7. Where the body was damaged

  or omitted the function, rather than guessing.
