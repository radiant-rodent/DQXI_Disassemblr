## What I did

Worked through all 150 functions of `orchestration/types/batches/V_113.json` in 19 `batchview.py V_113 next`

- `maybe_Battle_Action_ShouldUpdateUnitModel`: `unit`, `currentUnit`, `unitHandle`, `callResult`, `alphaBlend`,

  `invalidMessageId` from the 0x24-byte two-slot loop and the 1.0f/0.0f/0xFFFF constants.

  `classRecord`, `actorClassRecord` from the tagged-handle table `g_actorAttachGadgetHandleMap`.
- The four `Field_*_Ctor_vt*` constructors: `gadget`/`sceneObject`, `staticVtable`, `descWord`, `invalidId`,
  `flagMask`, plus `maybe_ctorArg`/`maybe_ctorArgLocal` for the `arg4` word forwarded by address to the base ctor.
- `maybe_Ui_PokerScoreList_SetDigits`: `divMagic`, `digitRow`, `digitIndex`, `remainingValue`, `isVisible`,
  `scratchValue` from the 0x66666667 divide-by-10 sequence and the six 0x50-byte rows.

## What I skipped and why

- 114 functions: 96 are `self`-only deleting destructors or `halt_baddata()` split-symbol fragments with no
  locals at all; the rest had only already-named parameters (`self`, `baseVtable`, `vtable`, `sprite`, `record`,
  `widget`, `key`, `listOwner`, `visible`, `flag`, `id`, `dt`, `textId`, `rowIndex`, `value`).
- `maybe_Field_SceneNode_BeginMeshLoad` (0x004C9BCC): only `unaff_r4/r5/r7`, `in_r12`, `in_lr` register
  artifacts in a bad-data fragment - nothing justifiable.
- `arg3` in the four `Field_*_Ctor_vt*` constructors: never read in the body, so left unnamed.
- `result` locals in `maybe_Gfx_SpriteObject_AttachToOwner`/`DetachFromOwner`, `maybe_Ui_ScrollBar_LookupRowText`
  and `Gfx_SpriteObject_UpdateCallbackSlots`: already named by the rule pass, left alone.

## Decisions recorded for the orchestrator

- Where one decompiler temp is reused for two different meanings I preferred an honest vague name
  (`scratchValue` in `Field_Gadget_Ctor_vt77833C` and `maybe_Ui_PokerScoreList_SetDigits`, `textValue` in
  `maybe_Ui_TalkWindow_SetText`) over a name that is right for only half the uses; the reuse is spelled out in
  `evidence`.
- Damaged-tail register artifacts (`in_pc`, `in_cr0`, `in_s0`, `in_s1`) were named after the registers they
  stand for (`pc`, `cr0`, `floatReg0`, `floatReg1`) at confidence 0.5-0.6 rather than guessed as game values.
