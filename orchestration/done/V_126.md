## What I did

Worked through all 145 functions the batch tool printed, in 18 chunks, answering each chunk with
`python3 tools/batchview.py V_126 --add -` and finishing with `--check` (145 answers, 0 problems).
`orchestration/types/out/V_126.json` parses as valid JSON.

- 50 functions received names: **234 locals** and **1 parameter** (`arg1` -> `a` in `std_Strncmp`).
- 3 of those names carry the `maybe_` prefix (all in `std_DoubleDivideCore`, confidence 0.45).

## Naming basis

Names came from the merged readable C: field names already present (`self->maybe_entry72c`,
`self->stateSequence`, `g_gspGpuServiceHandle`, `g_actorAttachGadgetHandleMap`,
`g_actorBaseClass`, `g_modelObjectVtable`), from the callee/argument roles in the body, and from
the vtable slots (`+0x14` request transition / `+0x10` commit, `+0x48` row getter, `+0x24` insert,
`+0x3C` selector dispatch). Recurring patterns got consistent names across the batch:
`transitionAccepted` / `transitionResult`, `rowCount` / `row` / `model`, `keyMask`,
`textRecord` / `animDescriptor`, `word0..word7` / `carryByte` / `shiftedWord` for the memcpy and
memmove family, `tlsBase` / `resultCode` / `serviceHandlePtr` for the IPC helpers.

  `Ui_TrainingWindow_DeletingDtor`, `Ui_WindowPart_DeletingDtor` — two calls, no locals.

  `maybe_Ui_TrainingWindow_UpdateSlotParts`, `maybe_Ui_LoadScreen_State3C`,

- **`maybe_Video_ConvertBlocks32_SamePlane`** (~90 locals) was left entirely unnamed: the printed

- **`maybe_Os_TranslateMainRamRangeB`** was left unnamed: a mid-function fragment with `unaff_r5`
