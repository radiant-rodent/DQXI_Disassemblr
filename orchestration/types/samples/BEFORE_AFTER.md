* `f_4c` / `f_50` -> `gaugePane` / `flashPane`, so the stores read as what they are;
* `g_00412aac` -> `&g_uiPartSentinel`: the Ghidra literal slot is resolved to the real

pipeline never renames a field it has no evidence for.
